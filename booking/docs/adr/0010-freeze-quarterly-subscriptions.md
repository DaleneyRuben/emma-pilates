# Let a client freeze a quarterly Subscription once

Status: accepted (2026-10-23)

A Subscription on a Plan of 90 days or more may be frozen once, for up to 15 days, never starting in the past. The client asks from Mi plan with dates and a reason; an Administrator approves or refuses it, prompted by a push, and the client is told. During the Freeze she cannot book, Bookings already inside it are cancelled with their class returned, and the end date moves by exactly the frozen days. A founder keeps her membership through a Freeze.

Approval stays with the Administrator because a Freeze changes what the studio owes; letting clients freeze alone would make the rule a right to extend.
