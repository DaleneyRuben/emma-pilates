# Include one Evaluation in quarterly Plans and charge for the rest

Status: accepted (2026-10-23); replaces «one free Evaluation a month with an active Subscription»

Evaluations were free to any client with an active Subscription, one per calendar month. The owner's rule: a Subscription on a Plan of 90 days or more includes **one** Evaluation, used inside its date range; every other client pays **Bs 70** (Studio Settings). The studio pays the Nutritionist **Bs 50** per Evaluation held, recorded as a cost.

- Only a client with an active Subscription may book, paid or included (owner, 2026-10-23).
- A Paid Evaluation is a Payment of its own (`kind = evaluation`) with no Subscription, by QR in the app (confirmed by an Administrator) or in cash at the front desk. While a transfer is pending the slot is held for 24 hours, then released (owner, 2026-10-23).
- A cancellation less than 3 hours before, or a no-show, counts the Evaluation as used; nothing is refunded.
- A confirmed Paid Evaluation cannot be cancelled (owner, 2026-10-29). The client reschedules it to another open slot until 3 hours before the original start. The Payment and the receipt stay with the Evaluation. No extra charge. If the studio or the Nutritionist cancels it, nothing is refunded: the Evaluation stays to be rescheduled and the client is told to pick another slot. A Paid Evaluation whose Payment is still pending cancels as before, and the Payment is rejected. An included Evaluation cancels as before.

Making Payment able to settle something other than a Plan is the larger change here; the alternative, a fake Plan for each Evaluation, would have put Evaluations in Plan reports and the Founder discount.
