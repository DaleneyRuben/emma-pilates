# Prompt S15 — Mi Agenda (Nutritionist)

Staff app, Nutritionist role. Chains from Prompt S01 (Login) — this is the home screen the Nutritionist lands on. Prompt S16 (Mi Disponibilidad) is a secondary path reached from this screen's header.

---

Using the Emma Pilates Studio design system (Prompt 00) and following on from Login (Prompt S01), design the **Mi Agenda** screen of the staff app, as seen by the Nutritionist — their home screen, showing who's booked into their upcoming Evaluations.

**Language:** all visible copy is neutral Spanish (Bolivia), informal "tú" address.

**Navigation chrome:** a header icon/button, "Mi disponibilidad", in the top-right corner, leading to Prompt S16. No tab bar (see design system's Staff App section).

**Layout, top to bottom:**
1. Greeting header: "Hola, [Nombre]" in the warm serif heading face.
2. A vertical list of upcoming Evaluation cards, grouped by day, each showing: time, and the client's name.

If there are no upcoming Evaluations, show an empty state: "No tienes evaluaciones agendadas."

Generous whitespace, soft-rounded cards per the design system.
