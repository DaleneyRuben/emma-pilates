# Follow-up — Admin web: structure proposals and v2 on the desktop

Targets: **the admin web shell** (`AW02`'s sidebar and top bar, `AW33`,
`AW34`) and a new **Inicio** page, as proposals only. Read with
[225](./225-visual-system-v2.md). No `AW` screen is regenerated by this
follow-up: the owner picks a structure and a direction first, then each page
is redrawn in a later follow-up.

---

Written on 2026-11-03 (DES-005). The owner says the admin web's menu and
organisation are a mess and asked for better information architecture and a
more modern, elegant look. An earlier 44-board v2 canvas was discarded at her
request; this starts from scratch.

## What the current admin web has

Studied on a local run at 1440 and 420 px (fixtures, Paola and Carolina):

- The sidebar has **25 entries**. «Back Office» alone holds 15 that mix
  clients, money, inventory and staff. At 1440 × 900, Reporte through
  Comentarios sit below the fold, and the sidebar's white ground stops at
  the first screen height.
- There is **no home**: `/` is Horario. Pending work (payments to review,
  freeze requests, clients to contact, low stock, pay statements to approve,
  new feedback) shows only as sidebar counts spread over five pages.
- The same thing lives in several entries: Pagos a instructoras and Pago a la
  coordinadora are one pay-statement view filtered two ways, and Nómina is the
  ledger of salaries paid; Indicadores and Recomendaciones are both monthly
  findings; Referidos repeats the client detail's block; Categorías de gasto
  is a sidebar entry of its own.
- Some pages are hidden: Reglas del estudio is only in the account menu;
  Retiros, Contactos para promociones and the campaign grant are sub-pages
  reachable from a link.
- The wordmark reads «Emma» only; the brand is «Emma Pilates Studio».
- The look is v1: white cards with a border and a shadow on Superficie.
- The Branch selector shows on studio-wide pages with a note that it does
  not apply.

## The proposals on the canvas

Three structures, each a clickable prototype, plus the visual direction and
the recommended one at 420 px:

- **A · By work area (recommended).** An **Inicio** page with one emphasis
  card (payments to review for the Administrator, clients to contact for the
  Coordinator), three figures, «Por resolver» rows that open the right page
  and tab, and today's classes. A short sidebar in groups: *Día a día*
  (Horario, Clientas, Pagos y caja), *Sede* (Insumos, Equipos, Nutrición),
  *Equipo* (Personal, Sueldos), *Finanzas* (Gastos, Reporte, Simulador),
  *Crecimiento* (Indicadores, Promociones, Comentarios), *Configuración*
  (Catálogo, Reglas del estudio). 17 entries instead of 25; merged pages
  become tabs:
  - Clientas: Todas · Para contactar · Congelamientos · Fundadoras ·
    Referidos (the campaign grant stays a button on Clientas).
  - Pagos y caja: Pagos · Cierres de caja.
  - Sueldos: Pagos a instructoras · Pago a la coordinadora · Sueldos pagados
    (today's Nómina).
  - Insumos: Stock · Retiros del mes. Gastos: Gastos · Categorías.
  - Indicadores: Indicadores · Recomendaciones. Promociones: Carrusel de la
    app · Contactos. Catálogo: Tipos de clase · Planes · Sucursales.
  The Coordinator sees Inicio, Horario, Clientas (Todas, Para contactar),
  Personal and Indicadores (ADR-0102). Studio-wide pages show «Todo el
  estudio» instead of the Branch selector.
- **B · Spaces on top.** Six spaces in a top bar (Hoy, Operación, Dinero,
  Comunidad, Análisis, Ajustes); the sidebar lists only the chosen space's
  pages. Close to today's pages, but two clicks to change area.
- **C · Icon rail and inbox.** Seven destinations in a narrow rail (Bandeja,
  Horario, Clientas, Dinero, Sede, Informes, Ajustes), everything else as
  tabs, a global search, and an inbox home with inline actions. The fewest
  entries, but long tab rows and less visible pages.

## The visual direction

v2 carried to the desktop, as 225 defines it: Marfil page, the sidebar and
every card, table and input in Lino with no border or shadow, the active page
in Rosa tinte with Rosa enlace text, one Rosa profundo emphasis per page,
Lora titles (page 32, card 20, figures 32 tabular) over Work Sans 15, section
labels in Work Sans 12 uppercase, buttons 44 high with 225's kinds (no
underlined links), tables as a Lino card with Arena hairlines, forms and
records in a side panel, the notice with a left bar and the Rosa profundo
toast. Sidebar 248 px; page margin 40 px (20 at phone width); the 225 spacing
scale. Brand «Emma Pilates Studio» everywhere, never the tagline.
