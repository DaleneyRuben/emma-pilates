# Follow-up — Plans by days in the Client catalogue

Targets: `08` (Elegir plan) and `09` (Detalle del plan). Send
[30](./30-invariants-checklist.md) and [175](./175-plans-by-days-admin.md)
alongside (175 amends the catalogue of checklist 30).

---

Written by BKM-005 on 2026-10-23 ([booking ADR-0005](../../../../booking/docs/adr/0005-validity-in-days-with-a-chosen-start-date.md),
[ADR-0006](../../../../booking/docs/adr/0006-weekly-cap-and-extra-classes.md)).
Validity is counted in days and the long Plans carry a Weekly Cap; no client
copy says «mes» or «meses» about a Plan.

## `08` Elegir plan

- The cards are the eight Plans of follow-up 175, in that order. Each card's
  terms line reads «{n} clases · válido por {d} días» («1 clase · válido por
  30 días» for the single class), and a Plan with a cap appends « · hasta {c}
  por semana» («16 clases · válido por 60 días · hasta 3 por semana»).
- «Más elegido» stays on one card: «8 clases».

## `09` Detalle del plan

- The header card's validity line reads «Válido por {d} días».
- Under «Qué incluye», the validity line reads «{d} días de vigencia desde
  que se confirma tu pago.» (BKM-006 later replaces this with the chosen
  Start Date; do not draw a date chooser now).
- For a Plan with a cap, a new line right after it: «Hasta {c} clases por
  semana.» Draw the sample frame on «Bimestral 16 clases» (Bs 1.120) so the
  line shows; keep one frame on «8 clases» (Bs 590) without it.

## Leave untouched

The price, the «Desglose» block and credit line, the cancellation line, the
payment-method note, the buttons, the tab bar, and every other screen.
