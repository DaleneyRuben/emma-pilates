## Correction to 257

From here on, read each option as **two screens**: a Mi plan hierarchy
and a freeze picker. 257's common Mi plan boards (M-Entrada, M-Revision,
M-Aprobado, M-Congelado, M-Rechazado, M-Sin-lugar) are replaced by one
interactive Mi plan board per option, whose control strip switches between
its states: **activo, por vencer, fundadora, congelamiento pedido,
aprobado, en curso, no aprobado, plan siguiente**. The pickers and the
refusal copy stay as 257 describes them.

## Why (finding 54)

With an approved freeze, Mi plan stacks a pink notice («Renueva antes
del…»), the maroon plan card, Detalles, a second pink notice («Tu plan está
congelado hasta…»), Renovación anticipada with its pink button and a colour
**Reservar una clase** at the bottom. The owner: «se ve con mucho color y
es muy difícil enfocarse, todo distrae». Each option gives Mi plan one
focal point and notices without coloured fills.

## The options

1. **A · La tarjeta es el foco** (recommended, with 257's month calendar).
   The maroon plan card is the only colour. Notices become a list without a
   fill, one icon each. The freeze is a neutral card with a status pill.
   Renovar and Congelar are chevron rows. No bottom button: **Reservar** is
   already on the tab bar.
2. **B · Tarjeta clara, una acción** (with the strip and number of days).
   The plan card turns Lino, without maroon. The one notice that matters
   is a line inside the card. The only colour is the bottom button:
   **Reservar una clase**, or **Renovar mi plan** while the plan is ending.
3. **C · Línea de tiempo** (with the «Desde»/«Hasta» fields and the sheet).
   A compact count on top, then the plan as a timeline: Empezó, Hoy, the
   freeze, the founder deadline, Vence (with «antes, el …» once a freeze
   moves it) and the next plan.

Common to all three: the founder notice reads «Renueva antes del … para
mantener tu 15 % de descuento.» (finding 49); a next plan that starts the
day after this one ends turns the freeze row off with its reason.
