# Follow-up — included and paid Evaluations

Targets: **13 (Evaluación nutricional)**, **14 (Confirmar evaluación)**,
**15 (Mis evaluaciones)**. Send
[30](./30-invariants-checklist.md) alongside this follow-up.

---

Written by NUT-003 on 2026-10-23 from decisions §6 and booking ADR-0011.
A Plan of 90 days or longer includes one Evaluation. Every other active
Plan pays the Studio Settings price (Bs 70 in the sample). A Client with
no active Plan still cannot book.

## `13` — the price on the slot

The intro no longer says the Evaluation is free with any Plan, or one a
month. It says a Plan of 90 days or longer includes one Evaluation, and
any other active Plan pays the price shown on the slot. Each bookable
turno names «Incluida» or «Bs 70». The no-plan frame stays.

## `14` — confirm included or paid

The included frame keeps «Es gratuita — no usa clases de tu plan.» The
paid frame replaces that line with «Cuesta Bs 70. La pagas por QR.»
Confirming a paid slot starts the existing QR payment review. Cash at
the front desk is not on this branch.
The reminders and the 3-hour cancel note stay.

## `15` — past labels

Anteriores no longer says «Realizada» on every row. The label is
«Incluida», «Pagada», «No asistió», or «Cancelada». A pending transfer
does not appear as history.

## Leave untouched

The Nutritionist card, the 45-minute length, the Branch name, and the
3-hour booking close.
