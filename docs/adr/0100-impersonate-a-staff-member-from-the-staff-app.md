# Impersonate a Staff Member from the Staff app

Status: accepted

An Administrator signed in to the Staff app may see and act in it as one active Trainer, Assistant, or Nutritionist for at most 30 minutes (FUT-001, [proposal](../implementation/FUT-001-impersonation-proposal.md)). [ADR-0030](./0030-use-opaque-server-side-authentication-sessions.md) left the scope of this feature open while requiring it to be «explicit server-side state»; this decision fills it in.

- **An Impersonation is a row, not a credential.** `system.impersonation_sessions` ties the Administrator's own mobile Authentication Session to the acting Staff Member with a start, an absolute expiry 30 minutes later, an end time, and an end reason (`exited`, `expired`, `logged_out`, `revoked`). The app keeps its one bearer credential. A session holds at most one active Impersonation.
- **The principal is swapped, not flagged.** While the Impersonation is unexpired, every request resolves to the Staff Member's Account, current Role, and current Branches ([ADR-0054](./0054-enforce-contextual-authorization-in-use-cases.md)), with `impersonation: { id, administratorAccountId }` beside it. Use cases therefore apply the Staff Member's rights unchanged, and the Administrator's rights are off for the duration; no business rule checks a flag.
- **Only an Administrator starts one, only from the Staff app, and never as another Administrator or a Client.** The Staff app is the only surface the impersonated Roles use, so the admin web has no entry and an admin-web session is refused.
- **Account-level self-service is refused while impersonating** (`403 impersonation_forbidden`): password and email changes, logging out other devices, push-device registration or deactivation, marking the Staff Member's notifications read, and a second Impersonation. Everything else the Role may do is allowed.
- **Every screen shows it, and it ends on exit, expiry, or logout.** A banner names the Staff Member and the minutes left and carries «Salir». Logout ends the Impersonation in the same transaction as the session's revocation; a deactivated Staff Member or a revoked session ends it on the next request.
- **Both people are in the audit trail.** `impersonation.started` and `impersonation.ended` are audited, and every audit record written meanwhile names the Administrator as actor and carries `impersonation_id`, through which the acted-as Staff Member is known. Unaudited rows keep the Staff Member's Account in their actor columns and are attributed by the Impersonation's time window.
- **The Staff Member is not notified.** The audit record is the record; a Perfil notice may come later.

Alternatives rejected: a second Authentication Session for the acting identity (two credentials to switch, two to revoke, and the Administrator's revocation would not end it), a client-side Role switch (authorization is server-side and the data shown would be the Administrator's), a per-request `Impersonate-As` header (no time box, no single exit, nothing for audit records to reference), and an admin-web hand-off into the phone (a browser-cookie to mobile-bearer crossing the session design keeps apart).
