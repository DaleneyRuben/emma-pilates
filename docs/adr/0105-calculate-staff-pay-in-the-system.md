# Calculate Trainer and Coordinator pay in the system

Status: accepted (2026-10-23); changes the Back Office rule that pay is worked out outside the system

A Salary Payment recorded only an amount and a date; how it was reached happened outside. The owner's pay rules depend on facts the system already holds, so it now drafts a monthly **Pay Statement** for each Trainer and for the Coordinator from dated **Pay Rate Rules**:

- Trainers: Bs 65 per taught Session with up to 4 students, Bs 8 per student from the fifth, counted on the Bookings held when the Cancellation Window closed (no-shows count); Bs 25 per Session cancelled below the Minimum Bookings; 10 % of what each Client she brought (her Referral Code) actually paid for her first Plan, in the month that Payment was confirmed.
- Coordinator: Bs 3,500 base; Bs 50 per new Client on a 30-day Plan and Bs 80 on a 60- or 90-day Plan, none for the single class, Gift Classes, or Guest Passes, and none when a Trainer brought the Client; a Bs 400 bonus when the month's Renewal Rate reaches 80 % (months 1–6 of operation), 85 % (7–12), or 90 % (from 13). The owner clarified (2026-10-28) that «renovó» is measured by purchase date: another real Plan whose Payment was approved, on the Bolivia date, before the previous Plan's end or up to 7 days after it, whatever Start Date she chose. Guest Passes and Courtesy Access are not renewals. The bonus and the KPIs use this one definition.

The Administrator approves a statement, which creates its Salary Payment. A Trainer sees only her own statement in the Staff app; the Coordinator sees none. Courtesy Access and store-review rows never count.
